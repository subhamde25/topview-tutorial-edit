# Brief (summary)

A freelance editor test from the Marcus AI Tool Hunt YouTube channel.

**The role:** remote, regular videos every month. Talking-head videos with screen recordings of AI tools, edited in the
style of [Malva AI](https://www.youtube.com/@malvaAI): zooms, captions, text pop-ups, logo animations, sound effects and
music. Paid per minute of final edited video.

**The test:** a short edit of about one minute, one of two options. This repo is **Option 1, Topview**:

- a script and a talking-head video of the presenter reading it;
- the Topview logo pack;
- log in to topview.ai with the test account provided, screen-record the steps in the script, and edit the talking
  head, recordings and logos into one video in Malva AI style.

**Rules:** the test is unpaid; use the tool's credits only for this test; don't change the account's password, email
or settings; submit within five days as a Google Drive (anyone with the link) or unlisted YouTube link.

**The script (Topview):** open topview.ai and log in → Canvas → add a node, Text to Video, model Topview T1 → paste a
prompt (a Japanese tea master pouring matcha) → 16:9, 10 s, 1080p, quantity 1 → Generate → while it renders,
AI Marketer: drop in a product link and auto-generate a short ad → back to Canvas: preview, download or regenerate →
summary: Canvas for control, AI Marketer for speed, a dozen-plus models under one roof.
